Call for Papers

6th Sailing the Macro Workshop - September 11-13, 2026

We are pleased to announce the upcoming sixth edition of the Sailing the Macro Workshop. We invite submissions from researchers, academics, and practitioners for original research papers covering various macroeconomics topics. The workshop aims to facilitate discussions of ongoing research on policy-relevant questions and provide a platform for exchanging ideas and findings among experts in the field.

May 14, 2026

Submission Deadline

Final deadline for all research paper submissions, by 12 CET.

June 8, 2026

Notification of Acceptance

Notification will be sent only to authors of accepted papers.

Program Publication

Conference Topics

The workshop welcomes submissions across the full spectrum of macroeconomics research. Topics include, but are not limited to:

Economic Policy

  • Monetary policy design and implementation
  • Fiscal policy and government spending
  • Policy coordination and international spillovers

Macroeconomic Theory

  • Business cycles & fluctuations
  • Firm dynamics & heterogeneity
  • Endogenous growth & innovation

International Economics

  • Exchange rate dynamics
  • International capital flows
  • Trade and global value chains

Financial Markets

  • Financial stability and systemic risk
  • Asset pricing and macroeconomic factors
  • Banking sector and credit markets

Emerging Challenges

  • Climate change economics
  • Global Supply Chains and Geopolitical Shocks
  • Artificial Intelligence and technological change

Econometric Methods

  • Time series and panel data techniques
  • Structural estimation and identification
  • Machine learning for economics

Paper Submission

Complete papers are preferred. All submissions must be in PDF format.
